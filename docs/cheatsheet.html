Targets (LFO, RANDOM, ENV MOD): pitch · OSC 2 · cutoff · resonance · level · FM · noise · drive · delay time · feedback. Noise: white · pink · brown · digital · metal · crackle. Drive: soft · hard · fold · fuzz · crush · decimate.

FEEDBACK above 100 % runs away into a roar. WIDTH 0 % mono, 50 % normal, 100 % extra wide. VELOCITY 0 %: every note full.

CLOCK ON + a MIDI clock: LFO and RANDOM rates become 8 BARS … 1/32 and delay times 1/32 … 1/1 (dotted and triplets too). START restarts the LFOs on the beat; no clock for 0.6 s: back to Hz and ms.

Saving a sound

SAVE → turn K1 to a USER slot (EMPTY or REPLACES) → SAVE. The sound then appears after the factory ones on the PRESETS knob.
